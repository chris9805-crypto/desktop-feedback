Privacy

Last updated 9 October 2026

IronBlock has no account, no server and no analytics. Your training log is stored on your own device and is never sent anywhere.

What the app stores

Everything you enter — your programs, logged sets, bodyweight readings, settings and the name you choose for yourself — is written to your browser's local storage on the device you are using. It stays there. There is nowhere else for it to go: the app has no backend, so there is no database holding your training, nothing to breach, and no copy of it that anyone else could be made to hand over.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups, logins or email addresses
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking
  • No cookies, advertising identifiers or third-party scripts of any kind
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location access
  • Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected

The only three times data leaves your device

All three happen because you chose to do them:

  • You save a backup file. The app writes a .json file to wherever you tell your device to put it. Where it goes afterwards is up to you.
  • You share a crew code. If you create one, it contains only a name you chose, your level, your streak and that week's session and set counts. Never your log, never your bodyweight, never anything you did not type into the name field. You send it yourself, to whoever you choose, through whatever app you like.
  • You load the page. Which brings us to hosting.

Hosting

The app is served as static files by GitHub Pages. Like any website, loading it means your browser makes a request to GitHub's servers, and GitHub receives what every web server receives: your IP address, your browser's user-agent string, and which files you asked for. That is GitHub's logging, not ours, and it is covered by GitHub's privacy statement. Nothing about your training is in those requests — the app is just files, and the logging happens before any of your data exists.

Once the app has loaded it works with no network at all. You can put the device in aeroplane mode and train a full session.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase everything removes it all immediately. Clearing site data for this page in your browser does the same thing. Neither needs our involvement, because we never had a copy.

The flip side is worth saying plainly: if you clear site data, lose the device, or leave the app unopened long enough in a browser that tidies up unused storage, the log goes with it and we cannot restore it. Save backups.

Children

IronBlock is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Since the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a new use of data.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or anything else: open an issue on GitHub.
